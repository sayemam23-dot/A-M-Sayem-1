🏆 1st Runner-up — Startup InnovateX

RentAll BD — a peer-to-peer rental marketplace for Bangladesh

Co-founder & CTO

Problem

People in Bangladesh own physical items — cameras, tools, event wear — that sit unused most of the time, while others who need those items only occasionally have no easy, trustworthy way to rent them.

Idea

A peer-to-peer rental marketplace, similar in spirit to Airbnb or Fat Llama, built for the Bangladeshi market.

Solution

A platform connecting owners of underutilised items with renters, backed by a trust layer so both sides feel safe transacting.

Target Users

Item owners looking to earn from idle possessions, and renters who need occasional access to items without buying them outright.

Key Features

Renter, owner and admin-facing app flows, and a 3-tier insurance/deposit trust system (Basic / Standard / Premium), modeled on platforms like Fat Llama and ToolMates.

Business Model

Seven planned revenue streams: transaction commission, insurance add-ons, delivery fee margin, owner subscriptions, featured listings, late/cleaning fees, and deposit interest.

My Role

Co-founder and CTO — working on product strategy, the trust/insurance system design, and the business plan.

Team

Sabiha Sultana Mouri (CEO), Raihanul Hasan (CMO), Samia Sheikh Sarah (COO), A M Sayem (CTO).

Outcome

Drafted a full business plan with user flows and unit-economics projections; currently preparing a 3-minute pitch, a one-page idea document, and a demo prototype built with Google AI Studio.

DIU Team Project

ধার দে (Dhar De) — a Bengali debt-tracking web app

Full-stack development · PHP / MySQL

Problem

Small, informal debts between friends and family in Bangladesh are usually tracked in someone's head or a notebook — easy to forget, and awkward to bring up once it's been a while.

Idea

A Bengali-language web app built specifically for tracking who owes whom, in a way that keeps the reminder social and a little playful rather than purely transactional.

Solution

A full-stack PHP/MySQL application where users log debts, settle them, and get nudged through gamified, non-awkward reminders.

Key Features

SmartSettle™ counter-debt netting (automatically cancels out debts owed both ways between two people), a "Shame Board" for overdue debts, gamification elements, and per-debt chat.

My Role

Full-stack development on the DIU team project — substantial iterative work on the PHP/MySQL application.

Tools

PHP, MySQL, and InfinityFree for free hosting/deployment.

Outcome

Built and iterated through several development rounds as a team project at DIU; hosting and deployment targeted on InfinityFree.
